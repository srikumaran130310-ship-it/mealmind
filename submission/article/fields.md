MealMind: An AI Meal Planner That Decides Before It Recommends

Description (max 512 characters):
MealMind is an AI meal planner that uses Strands Decider 2B to classify every candidate meal as allow, review, or block before recommending it. Built with Amazon Bedrock and Strands Agents, it helps students, families, and busy individuals cook practical meals from what they already have, within their budget and dietary restrictions.
